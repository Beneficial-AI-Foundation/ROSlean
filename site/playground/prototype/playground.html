What is imitated. Mux: the live input with the highest priority is forwarded; an input is live for 0.5 s after its last message; an engaged lock blocks every input below its priority. Monitor: 4 or more lidar points in the stop zone give zero; otherwise 4 or more in the slowdown zone give the command times 0.4; zero is published for 1 s and then nothing. Neither node sends a stop when it has nothing to forward: a watchdog in the base stops the robot when no command has arrived for 0.5 s, as in the project's Gazebo demo. In a stop zone every command becomes zero, turning included: drive into a box and you cannot steer out.
