  exist**: `output_eq_publish_pass_iff`, `output_eq_publish_slowdown_iff`,
  `run_silent_last_published_zero`, `run_scan_output`.
  `grep -rn output_eq_publish_pass_iff ROSlean core` finds nothing; the
  names are in `ros/roslean_collision_monitor/demo/gazebo/monitor_narrator.py`
